# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Expected behavior includes respectful communication, constructive feedback, and consideration for differing experience levels and perspectives. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and unwelcome conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when conduct violates these standards. Report concerns privately through the security contact configured for this repository on GitHub.
